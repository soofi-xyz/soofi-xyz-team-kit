# Archived primitives plan

Moved out of the live art direction when rendering moved to an image model. Kept so the constraints are not lost.

Phase 3 was going to propose three human/agent pairs on a primitives contact sheet. Miranda would pick one, and that pair would be written to `lexicon.json` as approved.

Every pair had to:

- Be distinct by silhouette alone, not just color. Tell them apart in grayscale and at 24px.
- Derive the agent form from the logo's prism geometry.
- Make the human form read as a person without being literal. No figures or icons.
- Sit on the 8px grid and connect cleanly to a connector primitive.

The stroke trial images were not shipped with the plugin. The archived geometry-svg skill is `art-direction/archive/geometry-svg.md`.
