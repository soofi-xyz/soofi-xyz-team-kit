# Page rhythm

Every section gets one primary visual. A section that ships as type only is a gap in the current site, not a rule. The inventory is evidence of style and of what a verb means. It is not a list of sections that may go without a graphic.

## Job, then goal

State the section's job in the page's argument before choosing a form.

- Makes a claim → emphasis. Pattern: focus.
- Explains how something works → clarity. Pattern: focus, unless the copy contrasts two or more things, in which case distinguishing.
- Moves the reader between ideas → flow. Pattern: background.

Clarity is not the default. A page runs at most two clarity sections in a row.

The hero carries the page's thesis. When that thesis is a claim, the hero is emphasis. The CTA bookends the page, and its job is to move the reader, so it is flow. Neighboring sections must not repeat the same goal and verb.

## Form

Choose a lexicon verb from that section's copy. Read its direction. Never use a verb against its direction.

- Pinch: constraint as a problem. `home.bottleneck-rays`.
- Meet: two things combining at a shared waist. `ai-native-work.venn-ray`.
- Rise: forward through stages. `method.hero-rays` is the style source for the verb. Do not copy that page's layout from it.
- Route: paths connecting parts that already exist. `home.bottleneck-group14`.
- Split: one source becomes several outputs.

Score the fit of the chosen verb from 1 to 5. Below 4, propose a new entry and mark it proposed. Do not force a seed verb into a section it only partly fits.

## Secondary marks

One primary per section. A secondary mark is an underline, a tick, or a connector. It goes on the words that carry the claim. It does not go on every item in a list. Skip the mark when the primary already carries those words.

Caps, counted from the inventory:

- Underline: 4 per section. `home.problem-underline` sits once under each of the four friction lines, which are the claim.
- Tick: 4 per section. `home.bottleneck-group13` is four strokes on those same four cards. The bottleneck rays do not get a tick per ray.
- Connector: 5 per section. `home.outcomes-connector` sits in each gap between the six artifact cards. Use a connector only when the primary does not already join those items.

The cap is a ceiling, not a quota.

## Ground

Indigo washes in the inventory (`home.hero-beam`, `method.hero-glow`, the about and workshops glows) are ground. Do not plan one as a second primary.

## Gaps

These shipped with no graphic. Plan a primary for each of them anyway.

- Home: plan, what-changes, not-outsourced, framework.
- About: the operating-model stance between the two fields.
- Workshops: the question list.
- Litepaper: the body sections between the top ray and the bottom glow.
- Method: recorded only as a holdout. Do not read the shipped method layout while planning that page.
